eink-qemu

An emulator for ESP32 e-ink readers: the Xteink X4 Pro, X3 and X4, and the BOOX Picco. It runs their unmodified firmware, stock or any fork, with the screen, SD card, keys and Wi-Fi behaving like the real board. This app runs native QEMU.

Built on QEMU. Started as a fork of prokrypt/x4prosim.

github.com/serialx/eink-qemu
Wi-Fi

Gives the emulated reader an open Wi-Fi network, PICSimLabWifi, on QEMU's user network, so the firmware reaches the internet and its font downloader works.

Applies when the emulator starts.

E-ink ghosting

Available on X3 and X4 Pro. X4 shows completed frames without ghosting.

Simulates faint traces of previous images and gradual fading, like a real e-ink panel. Turn it off for clean, stable pixels when comparing screenshots.

Applies when the emulator starts.

Turbo (fast, not timing-accurate)

Runs the device as fast as your computer allows: SD card, screen refresh and SPI transfer delays are cut to a minimum. Booting and page turns take a fraction of the time. Idle time still passes in real time, so a resting device keeps its screen and your CPU stays free.

Timing no longer matches real hardware, so log timestamps and refresh times are not realistic. Turn it off to see how fast the firmware runs on an actual device.

Applies when the emulator starts.

SD card

Drop files or folders here to copy them into the open folder. Changing the card while the emulator runs resets the device so it mounts the card again.
