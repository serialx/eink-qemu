eink-qemu

An emulator for ESP32 e-ink readers: the Xteink X4 Pro, X3 and X4, and the BOOX Picco. It runs their unmodified firmware, stock or any fork, with the screen, SD card, keys and Wi-Fi behaving like the real board. Everything runs here in your browser.

Built on QEMU. Started as a fork of prokrypt/x4prosim. Runs in the browser on Kohei Tokunaga's qemu-wasm JIT.

github.com/serialx/eink-qemu
Wi-Fi downloads

Gives the emulated reader an open Wi-Fi network, PICSimLabWifi, so the firmware's font downloader works. On the device open Settings › Reader › Manage Fonts and join it.

The fonts come from a catalog bundled with this page and are served by your browser: nothing goes through a relay server. Installed fonts are saved on the SD card.

Applies when the emulator starts. ?wifi=off opens the page with it off.

E-ink ghosting

Available on X3 and X4 Pro. X4 shows completed frames without ghosting.

Simulates faint traces of previous images and gradual fading, like a real e-ink panel. Turn it off for clean, stable pixels when comparing screenshots.

Applies when the emulator starts. ?ghosting=0 opens the page with it off.

Turbo (fast, not timing-accurate)

Runs the device as fast as your computer allows: idle waits are skipped and SD card, screen refresh and SPI transfer delays are cut to a minimum. Booting and page turns take a fraction of the time.

Timing no longer matches real hardware, so log timestamps and refresh times are not realistic. Turn it off to see how fast the firmware runs on an actual device.

Applies when the emulator starts. ?turbo=0 opens the page with it off.
